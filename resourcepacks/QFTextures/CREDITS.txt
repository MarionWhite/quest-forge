Quest Forge Textures (v1)
=========================

A resource pack for the Quest Forge modpack (Minecraft 1.7.10). Free, never sold or paywalled.
Built by tools/textures/build_qf_textures.py from four sources:

1. Faithful 64x -- https://faithfulpack.net
   Pack: "Faithful 64x", Java Edition 1.7.10 branch,
   https://github.com/Faithful-Resource-Pack/Faithful-64x-Java (branch 1.7.10,
   commit 89f200c, 2026-10-25).
   What we use (unchanged except a small colour-saturation lift, chroma x1.10):
     - block textures: stone; stone bricks (plain, mossy, cracked, carved); cobblestone and
       mossy cobblestone; all ores; gold, iron, diamond, emerald, lapis, redstone and coal
       blocks; glowstone; glass, the 16 stained glasses and the pane tops; dirt, grass_side and
       grass_side_overlay; farmland;
     - the connected-glass tiles (assets/minecraft/mcpatcher/ctm/glass/), with their rules
       rewritten for 1.7.10 block ids.
   Licence: Faithful License, Version 4 -- https://faithfulpack.net/license
   An unmodified copy is included as LICENSE-Faithful.txt.
   This pack is not made or endorsed by Faithful.

2. Vanilla Normals Renewed, by Poudingue (Thomas Cusson) --
   https://github.com/Poudingue/Vanilla-Normals-Renewed-1.12
   (1.12 v0.04, commit c848329; also https://www.patreon.com/Poudingue)
   What we use: its normal (_n) and specular (_s) maps, converted to the LabPBR 1.3 layout
   (green flipped to DirectX, ambient occlusion from its height, metal flags to LabPBR metal
   codes), on the textures that stay vanilla; on Faithful 64x textures its shapes are upscaled
   and combined with fine relief we generate. None of its colour textures are used.
   Terms (its README): free to modify and redistribute if not sold and with a link to its page.

3. Minecraft 1.7.10 (Mojang Studios) -- every other block texture, read from the
   player's own minecraft.jar when the pack is built (never stored in the repo),
   with the same saturation lift.

4. Quest Forge -- drawn by our own code (tools/textures/qf_art.py, qf_items.py): leaves,
   bark and log ends of all six woods, tall grass, fern, wheat, the grass top, bookshelves,
   potion bottles and enchanted-book covers; the build script; the generated LabPBR maps for
   everything the two packs above do not cover.
   Inspiration only (nothing copied, traced or recoloured): Stay True (plants and leaves),
   Soartex (bookshelves), Faithless (potion bottles and book covers).
