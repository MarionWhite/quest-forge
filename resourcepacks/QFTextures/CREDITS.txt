Quest Forge Textures (v0)
=========================

A resource pack for the Quest Forge modpack (Minecraft 1.7.10). Built by
tools/textures/build_qf_textures.py from three sources:

1. Faithful 32x -- https://faithfulpack.net
   Pack: "Faithful 32x", Java Edition 1.7.10 branch, release R17 (December 2025),
   from https://github.com/Faithful-Resource-Pack/Faithful-32x-Java
   What we use (unchanged except a small colour-saturation lift, chroma x1.12):
     - block textures: stone; all ores (coal, iron, gold, diamond, emerald, lapis,
       redstone, quartz); gold_block, iron_block, diamond_block; glowstone;
       glass, the 16 stained glasses and all glass-pane tops;
     - the connected-glass rules and tiles (assets/minecraft/mcpatcher/ctm/glass/).
   The normal (_n) and specular (_s) maps for these textures are generated by us
   from Faithful's art.
   Licence: Faithful License, Version 4 -- https://faithfulpack.net/license
   An unmodified copy is included as LICENSE-Faithful.txt.
   This pack is not made or endorsed by Faithful.

2. Minecraft 1.7.10 (Mojang Studios) -- every other block texture, read from the
   player's own minecraft.jar when the pack is built (never stored in the repo),
   with the same saturation lift. grass_top and grass_side_overlay are vanilla's
   pixels doubled to 32x with their contrast lowered.

3. Quest Forge -- the build script, the calmer grass treatment, and the generated
   LabPBR 1.3 normal and specular maps (https://shaderlabs.org/wiki/LabPBR_Material_Standard).

Free, never paywalled.
