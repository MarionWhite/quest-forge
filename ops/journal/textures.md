# Textures journal (newest first)

- 2026-10-26: r009 crops (step 3) closed at v2e. Stay True, the owner's crop pack, is ARR and has no local copy; CurseForge blocks scripted downloads, so the design worked from r008's frames, not from its pixels. v1's wheat was a solid chunky wall. wheat() now draws 5 front and 3 back plants with air between them: 1 px bowed stalks, arching blade leaves, and slim braided ears (5/7/9/10 px) that nod when ripe, in a brighter WHEAT_RIPE palette. Carrots and potatoes now use Faithful 64x's, from the farm the owner rated best (C). Render 1026-230307 (v2d): airier, but the ears were blobs on posts. Render 1026-231132 (v2e): the ears read as wheat heads. Still behind Faithful: the stalks are fairly straight, and stages 1-2 read as flat lime "+" shapes from above. Hand-drawn wheat masters are the next path if the owner wants Stay True's hand-placed look.

- 2026-10-26: r009 grass (step 2) closed at v2c. v1's drawn grass top had only 6 grey values and a diagonal swoosh repeating every tile, so it read as felt. The new grass_top_from() takes vanilla's own grass_top and keeps its pattern, which is what the owner liked in r007. It lowers only the pixel-to-pixel contrast, doubles each pixel with hard edges and adds a blade-tip sub-pixel. Render 1026-225431, t9v2c vs t9v1: the near ground is crisp again. At grazing angles the top is mip-smoothed in every version. The grass side stays Faithful 64x, whose dirt the owner liked.

- 2026-10-26: r009 leaves (step 1) closed at v2b. In leaves(), each clump now has 3 value groups and leaves sit only on crowns and silhouettes, with round see-through pockets (render 1026-223656). The hedge crop then showed a diagonal hatch: every crown was a straight cut toward one light. Crowns are now round highlights pushed toward a per-clump jittered light (render 1026-224756). The masses read and the hatch is down to a faint trace. This tunes the existing generator; if the owner still rejects the leaves, hand-painted leaf masters come next.
