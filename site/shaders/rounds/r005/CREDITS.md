# Credits: round 5 reference images

The `ref-*.jpg` files in this folder are real-world reference photos from Wikimedia Commons, shown next to
our renders. Each is a web-sized copy (at most 1600 px wide) of the original, otherwise unmodified. All are
under Creative Commons licences (or CC0) that allow reuse; the page shows the credit and a link under each
image. Files under CC BY-SA keep that licence. All were used in earlier rounds, where their licences were
checked on their Commons pages.

No screenshots of other shader packs or texture packs are shown. All other images here (`<scene>-<option>.jpg`)
are our own in-game renders.
