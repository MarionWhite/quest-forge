# Credits: round 4 reference images

The `ref-*.jpg` files in this folder are real-world reference photos from Wikimedia Commons, shown next to
our renders or as pick-only options, so a direction choice doesn't depend on how well an early shader draws it.
Each is a web-sized copy (at most 1600 px wide) of the original, otherwise unmodified. All are under Creative
Commons licences that allow reuse with credit; the page shows the credit and a link under each image. Files
under CC BY-SA keep that licence.

No screenshots of other shader packs or texture packs are shown in this round: the water-direction and
texture-pack questions only link to those projects' own pages. All other images here (`<scene>-<option>.jpg`)
are our own in-game renders.
