# Credits: round 1 reference images

The `ref-*.jpg` files in this folder are real-world reference images from Wikimedia Commons, shown
next to our renders so a taste choice doesn't depend on how well an early shader draws it. They are
the same images as in round 0 (`../r000/`), re-used under new names; each is a web-sized copy (at
most 1600 px wide) of the original, otherwise unmodified. Every one is public domain or under a
Creative Commons licence that allows reuse with credit; the page shows the credit and a link under
each image. Files under CC BY-SA keep that licence.

All other images here (`<scene>-<option>.jpg`) are our own in-game renders.
