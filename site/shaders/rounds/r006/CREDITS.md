# Credits: round 6

**Our renders** (`<scene>-<option>.jpg`) are our own in-game frames. Since this round they use the
**Faithful 32x** resource pack for Minecraft 1.7.10 (release R17, December 2025) by the Faithful
team, https://faithfulpack.net, under the Faithful License v4 (https://faithfulpack.net/license).
Faithful covers vanilla blocks only; modded blocks show their own 16x textures. Frames from round 5
(marked "round 5") use the vanilla textures.

The **night-sky B** and **night-new C** renders include the Milky Way from **ESO/S. Brunier**,
"The Milky Way panorama" (https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/), under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified for the shader pack: cropped to galactic
latitude -45 to +45 degrees, point stars removed, sky background subtracted, levels normalised and
resized; no AI was used.

The `ref-*.jpg` files are real-world reference photos from Wikimedia Commons, each a web-sized copy
(at most 1600 px wide) of the original, otherwise unmodified. Files under CC BY-SA keep that licence.
No screenshots of other shader packs or texture packs are shown.
