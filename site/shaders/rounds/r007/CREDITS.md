# Credits: round 7

**Our renders** (`<scene>-<option>.jpg`) are our own in-game frames. The shader questions use the
vanilla Minecraft textures again, except where noted below.

Some frames use the **Faithful 32x** resource pack for Minecraft 1.7.10 (release R17, December 2025)
by the Faithful team, https://faithfulpack.net, under the Faithful License v4
(https://faithfulpack.net/license):
- `lake-sunset-B` and `night-sky-B` are round 6 renders, which used Faithful 32x everywhere it has a texture;
- `tex-wall-B`, `tex-glass-B`, `tex-forest-B` and `tex-golden-B` use Faithful 32x;
- `tex-wall-C`, `tex-glass-C`, `tex-forest-C`, `tex-golden-C` and all four `pbr-*` frames use our test pack
  QF Textures v0: the vanilla textures, Faithful 32x for stone, the ores, the gold, iron and diamond blocks,
  glowstone and glass, and normal and specular maps we generated ourselves.

Faithful covers vanilla blocks only; modded blocks show their own 16x textures. The `tex-*-A` frames
also show Angelica's built-in default connected glass (textures by DarkBum, CC BY-SA, per the Angelica
README).

The **night-sky B** render includes the Milky Way from **ESO/S. Brunier**, "The Milky Way panorama"
(https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/), under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modified for the shader pack: cropped to galactic
latitude -45 to +45 degrees, point stars removed, sky background subtracted, levels normalised and
resized; no AI was used.

The **Faithless** questions (`fl-*`) only link to the pack's own pages and to images in its own
Modrinth and CurseForge galleries. Faithless is all rights reserved: none of its images are copied,
mirrored or shown on this site. The packs list (`tex-packs`) is links only too.

The `ref-*.jpg` files are real-world reference photos from Wikimedia Commons, each a web-sized copy
(at most 1600 px wide) of the original, otherwise unmodified. Files under CC BY-SA keep that licence.
No screenshots of other shader packs or texture packs are shown.
