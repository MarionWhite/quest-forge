Lucky Block 5.1.0 · Luck and odds

Break a Lucky Block

Break it for one of about 300 outcomes: gear, ores, mobs, traps, explosions, and a few messages. The pack removed the boss-tier jackpots. Crafting ingredients raise its luck, and rotten flesh lowers it.

The block starts at 0. Each crafting ingredient adds its value, and the total is clamped to −100 to 100.

How the mod weights an outcome

Every outcome has a chance from its config line (@chance, 1 by default) and a luck value (@luck, 2 by default). At luck L the weight of an outcome is its chance times b to a power, where b = 1 / (1 − 0.0077 × |L|). For L of 0 or more the power is the outcome's luck above the lowest one, and for L below 0 it is the highest luck minus its own. The weights are then divided by their total. This is the formula in ChooseLuckyDrop in the Lucky Blocks 5.1.0 jar.
