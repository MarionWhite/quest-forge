Day one, in order

  1. Open the quest book with '. Chapter 1 is Start. Claim the reward for Welcome to Quest Forge: 16 cooked beef, two BiblioCraft lanterns, a bed.
  2. Read Level Up classes before you press L for Pick a Class. The choice cannot be undone.
  3. Read what spawns below Y 40 before you dig there.
  4. Learn the voice keys: Tab talks, U mutes. See Playing together.

What is different at night

Infernal names, Weeping Angels, and the giants of the overworld. None of them spawn the way they do in vanilla.

Infernal names

Odds
One mob in 15 is at least Rare. One Rare in 7 becomes at least Ultra. One Ultra in 7 becomes Infernal. Roughly one mob in 735 ends as Infernal.
Modifiers
All 27 types are on. The table below says what each one does. Read the modifiers on the nameplate before you commit.
Damage cap
A modified mob has extra health, and no single hit takes more than 10 off it (maxOneShotDamage). Big weapons do not one-shot them; count on a longer fight.
Rare drops
Iron tools and armour, chainmail, a cookie.
Ultra drops
Bow, iron hoe, iron and golden armour, golden apples, blaze powder, enchanted books.
Infernal drops
Diamond tools and armour, chainmail, ender pearls, enchanted books.

Weeping Angels

Spawns
Below Y 40, at light level 8 or less.
Health
20.
Damage
Only a pickaxe hurts them.
Movement
They move only while nobody looks at them.
Teleport
35 percent of hits move you 5 to 25 blocks away.
Infection
10 percent of hits.
Names
They take a player's name, and they throw sounds.
Not taken
Your inventory. They do not knock down light, so your torches stay.
Practical
Light the mine and keep a pickaxe in the hotbar before you go below Y 40.

Overworld giants

Mutant Creatures, Lycanites Mobs and OreSpawn's giants roam the overworld. OreSpawn's random overworld dungeons are switched off. A giant in the open is a reason to leave, not to fight.

Difficulty by place

Status
Built and shipped inert. Until a tier grid is in the world, nothing changes. Tuning waits for the author's approval.
Tiers
T2 to T6, set by map position. There is no T1. T2 is the spawn pocket, within about 650 blocks of a spawn. T3 is most of the land, T5 the hot zones, T6 the outer sea.
Spawns
Ten spawn points, each rated Gentle, Hard, Brutal or Deadly by the land around it. The picker is not in the game yet.
Pockets
Once the grid is in a world, Lycanites event mobs do not spawn inside a spawn pocket. This is the one rule that acts by default.

When you die

What the pack adds to vanilla death.

Gravestone
Everything you carry goes into a gravestone where you fell. GraveStones mod.
Opening it
Friends can open it too. The mod has no owner-only setting, so tell them where you fell.
Respawn
Your last bed. The gravestone does not respawn you, and /home does not change it.
Skill points
Five percent are lost. Your class is kept.
Designs
A new gravestone design unlocks every 5 levels. Pick it with M.
Sleep
In the pack, every player in the dimension must be in bed to skip the night; the pack has no sleep mod. The friends' server votes at half, rounded down. See Playing together.

Level Up classes

Press L. A class adds skill points to three skills at once. Quest 431, Pick a Class, names Miner, Warrior and Scout.

Points
Automatic. A class adds 10 points to its first skill, 5 to its second and 5 to its third. Freelancer adds 20 skill points instead.
Extra XP
Each class also names an activity that earns extra XP (mining, combat or crafting). This pack turns those bonuses off, so only the points apply.
Cap
25 points per skill.
Undo
Quest 431 says this build has no Book of Unlearning. Nothing in the pack config says a class can be changed.
Death
The class survives death. Skill points are reduced by five percent. See When you die.

Items and luck

Things worth knowing on day one, and where to open them.

ItemWhat to know
Lucky BlockBreak it for one of about 300 outcomes: gear, ores, mobs, traps, explosions. Craft it with diamonds, gold or emeralds to raise luck; rotten flesh or spider eyes lower it. The pack removed the boss-tier jackpots. Quest: Feeling Lucky? Break one on this site first.
Loot BagRight-click for 1 to 5 random items from the pack's chest pools, better at higher tiers. Five tiers, Common to Legendary. Bags never contain Quest Forge items. Mobs drop one only when a player kills them. The Loot Recycler takes unwanted items and outputs a Common bag. Quest: Loot Bag Basics.
Pandora's BoxPlace it and open it for up to three random world effects, slightly more often bad than good: terraforming into Nether, End or snow, item showers, mob armies, TNT, lightning, lava cages. Open it away from your base. Found in dungeon, mineshaft, temple and stronghold chests. Quest: Pandora's Box.
Sealed TomeRight-click to break the seal: it becomes an enchantment book of its rarity, Common to Legendary. Drop the book onto an item; the tooltip gives the chance it takes and the chance it destroys the item. Magic Dust adds five points. Enchantments has the detail.
Adventure Backpack39 slots, two tool slots and two fluid tanks. Open it on the move with B. Bat skin sees in the dark; Squid skin breathes underwater.
JukeboxCraft a blank record from coal, string and redstone. Right-click the jukebox to search music; sneak-right-click to play or stop it. Your own mp3 or ogg files go in the jukebox folder of the game directory. A blank record in the jukebox presses a song onto it.

Tickets and commands

Ordinary players have no teleport commands. A ticket unlocks one, for good.

Use
Right-click a ticket. /commands lists what you have.
Home
/sethome and /home: one home, reachable from any dimension. It does not change your respawn.
Vault
/pv: a private 54-slot vault that exists nowhere in the world. Its contents follow you through death and dimension changes. It is not an ender chest.
World tickets
1 in 5000 from any mob a player kills.
Dimension tickets
The way back into a dimension. They drop only inside it: 1 in 10 from a boss there, 1 in 3000 from any other kill there.
Planned
/tpa on the friends' server, as a ticket unlock like /home (decision 0007). Not in the pack yet.

Playing together

Parties, voice chat, and shared storage.

Party
The pack's quest book has a Party tab. The friends' server has no parties (below). Decided for boss hunts: everyone who damaged the boss gets the credit (decision 0004).
Talk
Tab to talk, U to mute. You hear a player within 40 blocks in the same dimension, at full volume inside 6 blocks, fading out at 40.
Voice commands
/voice shows your status. /voice mode open switches to an open mic. /voice level and /voice threshold <n> tune it; the threshold runs from 0 to 1.
EnderStorage
A chest shares its colour code with everyone, unless it is locked with a diamond (personalItemID=diamond). It is a mailbox until you lock it.

The friends' server

Decided 2026-10-25 (decision 0007). These are the server's settings, not the pack's.

  1. PvP: as the pack has it. One team is not used, and friendly fire stays on.
  2. Quests: personal. No parties.
  3. Sleep: the night skips when 43 percent of players are asleep, which is half of the players, rounded down.
  4. Idle: you are kicked after 30 minutes.
  5. Spawn: 16 blocks of spawn protection. The hub at spawn is a design proposal, with no portals.
  6. Loaders and machines: Railcraft world anchors cannot be crafted. Sync does not load chunks. A Chest Transporter cannot carry spawners. The Time Watch pedestal bonus is lower. Lycanites events start on day 2 or 3. Operator loaders stop when their owner is offline.
  7. Graves: the defaults. Nobody is told not to loot graves.
  8. Map: no world border. Opis is on. /tpa is planned as a ticket unlock.

The climb

Iron, then diamonds, then the Twilight Forest.

Chapter 1 ends at Diamond Gear. The first step out is Twilight: The Pool at Dusk: dig a 2 by 2 pool in open grass, ring every edge with flowers, throw in a diamond, and step away from the water.

The numbered chapters, and what has to die before each one opens, are on the homepage. The worlds page covers the dimensions, the item index covers every item, and enchantments covers the tomes.
