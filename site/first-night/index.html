Your first night

The first night is not a fight. It is a test of walls. This is what to do in the first hour, what is out there after dark, and how to keep the things you pick up.

Before dark

Five jobs, in this order. Do them before the light goes.

  • Note where you woke. Press F3 and write the coordinates down on paper, not in your head.
  • Open the map. Press J. The full-screen map remembers everywhere you walked and marks where you died.
  • Wall the place you sleep. Hostile mobs spawn in the dark, so a wooden door and 8 torches are the minimum. Zombies walk through an open door.
  • Get food. Hunger never stops. Plant the seeds grass gives you and bake bread.
  • Make a bed. You respawn at your last bed. The reward for Welcome to Quest Forge includes one, and it waits for you.

The quest book opens with the apostrophe key, '. Start with Welcome to Quest Forge, then the Start chapter: Punch Some Trees, Crafting Station, Better Tools, Diamond Dreams.

I built a beautiful house and forgot the door. It was beautiful for about a minute.Mason

What comes out at night

Zombies in numbers, and four kinds of thing you should not meet yet.

Every night

Zombies at the door

They walk through an open door. Fight from the doorway, and step back inside when you are under half health.

Roughly one mob in fifteen

Infernal names

A long name and extra modifiers: more health, more powers, enchanted loot. The quest The Name Was Longer asks you to survive one.

Below Y 40, light level 8 or less

Weeping Angels

They move only while nobody looks, and only a pickaxe hurts them. A hit can teleport you 5 to 25 blocks away, or start an infection (10 percent). Light your mines, keep a pickaxe in the hotbar, and stay out of the dark below Y 40 until you have iron. They do not steal your inventory.

Overworld, in the open

Giants and mutants

Mutant Creatures, Lycanites Mobs and OreSpawn's giants roam the overworld. OreSpawn's random overworld dungeons are switched off in its config. If it is taller than your house, walk away.

Five is a good night. Six is when you learn to count.Gate Log

When you die

Your things go into a gravestone where you fell.

  • Walk back and open the gravestone. Everything you carried is inside it.
  • Respawn is at your last bed, not at the grave. Sleep in one before any trip.
  • Your friends can open it too. Tell them where you fell rather than hoping they look.
  • Level Up keeps your class but takes five percent of your skill points. No skill holds more than 25 points.
  • Everyone must be in bed to skip the night. Every player in the dimension counts. No sleep mod is installed.

We found the grave. Then we found the creeper that made it. In that order.Recovery Team

Playing together

Parties, voice chat, and a few things that are safer with friends.

  • Parties. Open the quest book, choose Party, and invite by name. A kill by any member counts for the party.
  • Voice chat. Hold B to talk and N to mute. You hear a player within 40 blocks in the same dimension, at full volume inside 6 blocks, fading to silence at 40. /voice shows your status; /voice mode open for an open mic; /voice level and /voice threshold <n> tune it.
  • EnderStorage. A chest shares its colour code with everyone unless it is locked with a diamond (the pack's personalItemID is diamond). It is a mailbox until you lock it, and a safe after.
  • Builds. Ask before you touch another player's build. Other server rules are not written down yet.

Everyone starts on the same page. Not everyone turns it.Keeper

Luck and loot

Things that are fun to open, and a few that are better opened away from home.

ItemWhat to know
Lucky BlockBreak it for one of about 300 outcomes: gear, ores, mobs, traps, explosions. Craft it with diamonds, gold or emeralds to raise its luck; rotten flesh or spider eyes lower it. The pack removed the boss-tier jackpots. Quest: Feeling Lucky?
Loot BagRight-click to open for 1 to 5 random items from the pack's chest pools. Five tiers, Common to Legendary, and mobs drop one only when a player kills them. A Loot Recycler pays out Common bags for what you no longer want. Quest: Loot Bag Basics.
Pandora's BoxPlace it and open it for up to three random world effects, a little more often bad than good: terraforming, item showers, mob armies, TNT, lightning, lava cages. Open it away from your base. Quest: Pandora's Box.
Sealed TomeRight-click to open an enchantment book. Drop the book onto an item. The tooltip gives the chance it takes and the chance it destroys the item. Magic Dust adds five points. Full detail on the enchantments page.
Adventure BackpackA chest you wear, with two fluid tanks. Open it on the move with B. The Bat skin sees in the dark and the Squid skin breathes underwater.
JukeboxCraft a blank record from coal, string and redstone. Right-click the jukebox to search music, sneak-right-click to play or stop it. Drop your own mp3 or ogg files into the jukebox folder of the game directory. Put a blank record in the jukebox to press a song onto it.

Tickets

Ordinary players have no teleport commands. A ticket unlocks one, for good.

  • Right-click a ticket to use it. /commands lists what you have.
  • /sethome and /home: one home, reachable from anywhere, even from another dimension. It does not change where you respawn.
  • /pv: a private 54-slot vault that exists nowhere in the world. Its contents follow you through death and dimension changes. It is not an ender chest.
  • Where they drop. Any mob a player kills has a 1 in 5000 chance to drop a world-loot ticket. A dimension ticket, the way back to a dimension, drops only inside that dimension: 1 in 10 from a boss there, 1 in 3000 from anything else.

Then what

The climb, in one line: iron, then diamonds, then the Twilight Forest.

Chapter one ends at Diamond Gear. The Twilight Forest is next. Make a 2 by 2 pool of water in open grass, ring it with flowers, throw in a diamond, and step back.

The numbered chapters, and what has to die before each one opens, are on the homepage. The worlds page covers the dimensions, and every item is in the item index.
