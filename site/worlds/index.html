Twenty-seven worlds

Every place the pack can take you, with the loading screen painted for it. Seven mods supply them, from the vanilla Nether to OreSpawn's Chaos, and the quest book decides the order you earn them in.

Vanilla2 Twilight Forest1 Witchery3 Welcome to the Jungle1 Compact Machines1 Legends Mod13 OreSpawn6

The order you earn them. The Nether comes first, with the overworld. The Twilight Forest opens at diamond gear, and Witchery's three realms sit behind the Circle Talisman. The End waits for the Hydra. OreSpawn's six open once you have found the Endstone Temple, and TragicMC's two once Mobzilla is dead. The Legends worlds, the Lost World and the inside of a Compact Machine are side roads: open whenever you find the way in.
