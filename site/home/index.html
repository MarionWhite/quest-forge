A hand-built questline

Twenty chapters. The numbered ones are the climb, and each opens when a boss dies: Witchery at the Naga, the End at the Hydra, TragicMC at Mobzilla. The rest are how you stay alive on the way up.

Light and air

The pack's own shaders, as they stand after six review rounds.

Judge the next round →

Near-photoreal light and air on unmistakably Minecraft geometry. Realism comes from the sky, the sun, shadows, water and atmosphere, never from smoothing away the blocks.

The pack runs on the Angelica client and ships with a Low and a Potato preset, so a GTX 1060-class PC is the floor rather than the exception.

  • Readable darkness: nights and caves are dark, never black.
  • Calm exposure, neutral colour, no orange wash at golden hour.
  • Motion blur, depth of field and lens flare are off unless you ask.
  • Every frame is our own render; reference photos in the Lab are credited.
