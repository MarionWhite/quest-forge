Eighty-one enchantments

None of them come from an enchanting table. You find a sealed tome, break the seal, and drag the book it becomes onto your gear. Every book rolls its own odds, and a bad roll can shatter the item. Scrolls tilt the odds; nothing removes them.

How it works

Tome to book to item. The roll happens in your hand, not in a chest.

1 · FIND

A sealed tome

One stackable item per rarity, in dungeon, mineshaft, pyramid, stronghold and village-blacksmith chests, and as quest rewards. It carries no odds yet.

2 · OPEN

Break the seal

Right-click it and it becomes a random enchantment book of that rarity, with a freshly rolled success chance and destroy chance written on it. Usually level one; a level three book is a find.

3 · ROLL

Drag it onto gear

Pick the book up in your inventory and drop it on the item. On a success it applies and the book is used up. On a failure the book is still gone, and the destroy roll decides whether the item goes with it.

Two books of the same enchantment are not the same book. A 78% / 10% Executioner is a better find than a 55% / 30% one, and the odds show on the tooltip before you commit.

Modded gear counts. Anything built on a vanilla sword, tool, bow or armour class is accepted, which is most of the pack; the rest is whitelisted by name.

They never roll at a table and never appear in vanilla loot books. The only way in is through a tome.

Multi Strike makes every proc enchantment on the same item fire twice. That is why there is one dispatcher behind all 81, and why it is Legendary.

Four ways to tilt a roll

All used the way books are: pick one up, drop it on the thing it should affect.

Drop on a book

Magic Dust

Raises the success chance by 5 points, as many times as you have dust, capped at 95%. The last five percent is the Binding Scroll's job.

Drop on a book

Ward Scroll

One shattering is absorbed. The ward is spent only on the roll that would have cost the item, so it never burns on a safe one. This is what makes a Legendary worth trying.

Drop on a book

Binding Scroll

The book becomes a sure thing: 100% success, 0% destroy. The rarest of the four.

Drop on an item

Solvent

Dissolves one of the item's custom enchantments, chosen at random. You don't get to pick, so it can't walk a Legendary from one item to a better one for free.

Tomes are weighted 12 / 8 / 4 / 2 / 1 from Common to Legendary in chest loot. Dust is common in the same chests; the scrolls are scarce, the Binding Scroll most of all. Quests hand out tomes directly, so the roll still happens in your hand.
