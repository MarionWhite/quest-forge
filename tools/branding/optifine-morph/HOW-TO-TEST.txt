------------------------------------------------
Recommended test (work-folder jar, keep shaders)
------------------------------------------------
1. Quit the game completely (Morph was moved while running last time;
   a leftover classload will confuse the test).
2. Copy ONLY this file into the live mods folder:
     QuestForge-Work\_morph-optifine-20260904\Morph-Beta-0.9.3-QF-OPTIFINE.jar
   Leave mods_disabled\Morph-Beta-0.9.3.jar where it is (do not also
   enable 0.9.1 Morph.jar). Leave iChunUtil.jar in mods\.
3. Do NOT change OptiFine. Do NOT turn Fast Render on.
4. Launch with TEST-LAUNCH.bat. In logs\latest.log confirm:
     Morph 0.9.3 loaded
     OptiFine_1.7.10_HD_U_E7
     [QuestForgeTweaks] Transform applied: PanelButtonQuest...
   There must be exactly one Morph jar in mods\.
5. New World or a throwaway copy. Turn Sildur on if you play with it.
6. Kill a vanilla mob you have never acquired (cow / pig / sheep).
   Watch for: stay in-world, acquire animation (black cube suck),
   morph appears in the radial menu.
7. Open the morph menu and become that mob. Look around, swing,
   switch to third person (F5), switch back.
8. First-person hand: you should still have a hand / held item.
   It may be the morph arm (guard working) or briefly vanilla.
9. Morph back to player. Kill a second new mob. Morph into it.

If any of these happen, quit and move the jar back to mods_disabled:
  - Kick: "A fatal error has occurred, this connection is terminated"
  - Freeze / hang on morph-in (watch for onRenderHand in latest.log)
  - Crash-report mentioning:
        GLAllocation.func_74523_b / deleteDisplayLists
        ModelHelper.getModelCubesCopy
        EventHandler.onRenderHand
        StackOverflowError / InvocationTargetException
