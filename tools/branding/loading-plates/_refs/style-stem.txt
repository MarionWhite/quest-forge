STYLE STEM — prepend to every scene. Do not edit per plate.

Minecraft rendered with a high-end path-traced shader pack (SEUS PTGI /
Complementary Reimagined quality), cinematic 16:9 still frame.

GEOMETRY: strictly cubic voxel geometry on a one-metre block grid. Every
surface is a flat block face with crisp 16x16 pixel-art texturing, hard
right-angle edges and visible block seams. Nothing is rounded, smoothed,
sculpted, bevelled or organic. Trees, creatures, terrain and architecture
are all built from whole cubes. It must read unmistakably as Minecraft.

RENDERING: photoreal lighting on that blocky geometry. Full path-traced GI
with coloured light bleeding between blocks, ray-traced reflections, soft
contact shadows and AO in every seam, thick volumetric fog with god-ray
shafts, atmospheric scattering and depth haze, heavy bloom on every
emissive block, gentle far-plane DoF, filmic tonemapping, fine film grain.

WATER: any water is a true mirror of the sky and the geometry above it.

TWO-LIGHT: cold ambient, one warm key — unless the plate variable says
otherwise. Nether is the exception: warm ambient, cold accent.

EXPOSURE: night, about 85% crushed shadow, only the warm source fully
open, blacks toward #07060B.

AVOID: smooth or rounded geometry, non-cubic low-poly, sculpted or organic
forms, cartoon flat shading, unshaded vanilla Minecraft, bright daylight,
flat overcast, washed-out grey fog, milky haze, desaturated pastel, neon
oversaturation, lens flare, realistic human-proportioned figures, text,
letters, UI, HUD, watermark, signature, borders, frames, split panels.

PALETTE: cold #F4ECFB → #9687A8 → #3D3445
         warm #FFB35D → #FA882F → #934217
         void #07060B

SAFE ZONES: top eighth and bottom quarter dark and uncluttered (dimension
name + progress bar).
