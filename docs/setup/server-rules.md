# Quest Forge server rules

Draft of the post for the friends, 2026-10-25. Short on purpose: it goes on a
BiblioCraft board at spawn and in the Discord. Every rule that waits on one of
the owner's decisions is marked `[pending: srv-xxx]`, with the recommended
wording filled in; when a decision lands, keep or replace the sentence and drop
the tag. The server settings behind each rule are applied with
`build_play_server.py --rules` ([`hosting-a-server.md`](hosting-a-server.md#the-owners-decisions-rules)).

---

**Welcome to Quest Forge.** It is a small server for friends. These rules exist
so nobody loses a week of work, and so the server stays fast enough for all of
us.

1. **Ask before you touch it.** Other people's builds, chests, machines, pets
   and vehicles are theirs. Griefing is only for consenting targets. Half the
   pack breaks blocks (ICBM, OreSpawn bosses, MCHeli, Lucky Blocks, Pandora's
   Box): aim them away from anyone's base.

2. **PvP.** `[pending: srv-pvp]` Everyone is on one team, so stray hits from
   Legends powers, helicopters, missiles and the Gravity Gun do not hurt your
   friends. Want a proper duel? Ask an op to take you both off the team for it.

3. **Quests are personal.** `[pending: srv-quests]` No quest parties. Lending
   someone the item a quest asks for is fine (the book does not take it), but
   earn your own big rewards instead of passing one set around.

4. **Sleep.** `[pending: srv-sleep]` When half the people online are in bed,
   the night skips. If it is night and you are busy, hop in a bed when asked.

5. **AFK.** `[pending: srv-afk]` The server kicks you after 30 minutes idle. An
   idle player keeps 169 chunks and a full share of monsters loaded all night.
   Log out instead.

6. **The hub at spawn.** `[pending: srv-hub]` Spawn is the shared place: the
   rules board, a community Ender Chest, the Twilight portal. The 16 blocks
   around it are protected. Build your own base elsewhere, and keep machines
   and farms out of the spawn chunks: they run 24 hours a day there.

7. **Chunk loaders.** `[pending: srv-loaders]` ChickenChunks loaders: up to 49
   chunks each of you, and they switch off while you are offline. Railcraft:
   use Personal Anchors (they run on ender pearls while you are online); World
   Anchors are limited to 20 per world. Sync shells do not keep chunks loaded.
   If your base needs more than that, ask.

8. **Graves.** `[pending: srv-graves]` When you die, a grave holds your things.
   Do not loot other people's graves. Death also costs 5 skill points, so
   bring a Sync shell or a bed before a boss.

9. **The things that melt the server.** `[pending: srv-tools]` Do not move mob
   spawners with a Chest Transporter. The Watch of Flowing Time in a pedestal
   gives no speed bonus here. Park Archimedes ships by decompiling them; a
   parked ship entity is how ships get lost.

10. **Ender Chests are shared.** Anyone who sets the same three colours opens
    the same chest. Diamond-lock yours for a private one; use the shared
    colours as mailboxes.

11. **Backups and restarts.** The world is backed up every hour and the server
    restarts every day at 05:00 (you get 10, 5 and 1 minute warnings). If
    something of yours vanishes, say when it happened; an hourly backup can
    bring it back.

12. **Stay on the current pack.** Install from the release link, never by
    adding mods yourself. If the server refuses you, read
    [why-was-i-refused.md](why-was-i-refused.md): it tells you which side is
    out of date.

---

## Not rules, but good to know

- The server is in online mode: your Minecraft name is your account's name.
- `/home`, the vault and dimension tickets are unlocked through qf-commands'
  lottery tickets. A `/tpa` unlock is planned.
- Voice chat is proximity-based and cuts at 40 blocks.
- Flight is allowed (Transformers, Legends, gliders), so flying is not a
  cheat report.
- Two known crashes (Legends lightning) restart the server within a minute.
  If it happens, wait, then reconnect.
