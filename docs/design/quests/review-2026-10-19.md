# Quest book review, 2026-10-19: the Prologue and the act spine

**For:** the pack author. **Status:** draft. Nothing is installed. The shipped book
(`pack/config/betterquesting/DefaultQuests.json`) and the server book are unchanged.

Three things are up for review:

1. **The four-act spine**: [act-structure.md](act-structure.md). Acts, chapters, boss gates,
   dimensions, what moves where, side chapters and party play. This is a plan; it is not built.
2. **The Prologue, "Arrival" (WP-5)**: built as a patch,
   `tools/quests/patches/wp5_prologue.py`, into a draft copy of the book,
   `tools/quests/drafts/DefaultQuests.wp5.json`. 17 quests: 10 new, 7 moved.
3. **A writing-pass sample (WP-10)**: the nine quests that were Ch 1, rewritten in the house
   voice of audit 4.7, in the draft only. The before/after table is in section 4.

## 1. What to look at, in this order

1. **The Arrival map** (section 2). Is this the first evening a friend should have?
2. **The rewards** (section 3), and the five marked for approval.
3. **The decisions** (section 5). Ten of them, most a yes or no.
4. **The voice** (section 4). Read two or three rows aloud. If the voice is right, WP-10 applies
   it to the rest of the book; if not, say what to change before it spreads.
5. **The act boundaries** in [act-structure.md](act-structure.md), sections 1 and 3.

To read the whole draft as text:

```
py -3.10 -I tools/quests/qb.py dump --book tools/quests/drafts/DefaultQuests.wp5.json --out wp5.txt
```

## 5. Decisions needed

Each has a recommendation; the draft follows it unless noted.

1. **Act boundaries.** As in act-structure section 1: Twilight splits at the Hydra (Naga to
   Hydra in Act II; Knight Phantom to Snow Queen in Act III); TragicMC stays whole in Act IV,
   and the Act III finale asks for Ultimate Gear instead of the TragicMC key that audit 4.1
   wanted. *Recommend: yes.* Not built: this is WP-6.
2. **One tab per act, or act-prefixed mod tabs?** One tab per act (`II: Old Powers` holding
   Twilight and Witchery side by side) gives each act a real ending on one screen.
   Prefixed mod tabs (`II: Twilight`, `III: Twilight`, `III: The End` …) keep today's layouts
   but double the tab count. *Recommend: one tab per act.*
3. **The `/home` ticket at 480 Into the Chronicle.** `/sethome` and `/home` for good, working
   across dimensions; today it is a rare world-loot find. It is the strongest thing in the
   Prologue: it adds no combat power, but it is a way out of any fight and any wrong
   turn, from any dimension. *Recommend: yes*, because a new friend on a server where
   everyone else has a base is exactly who it helps. Fallback: 1 Common loot bag.
4. **The jukebox as a quest reward.** No recipe registers `qfcontent:qf_jukebox`, so in
   survival 476 Bring Your Own Music would be its only source, once per player. Either keep
   it a quest reward (one per player; friends share by visiting) or give it a recipe in
   qf-content. *Recommend: a recipe as well*, so a second jukebox for a second base is
   possible; the quest still hands out the first.
5. **Parties and the Prologue.** If BQ copies a party's completed quests to a friend who
   joins it (likely, unverified: WP-9), a late friend who joins the party at 478 skips the
   Prologue they most need. Options: (a) 478 tells newcomers to finish Arrival before joining;
   (b) accept it, since the party can explain; (c) move 478 to the finale. *Recommend: (a)*,
   once WP-9 confirms the behaviour. The draft does not do this yet.
6. **Prologue length and the gate.** 17 quests, about 20–30 minutes, and Act I waits for 480
   (473 First Night + 474 graves + 478 party; the other 14 are optional or on the way). A
   leaner 12 would drop 472, 475, 476, 477 and 61 into side tabs. *Recommend: keep 17*; the
   checkboxes take seconds, and a veteran can clear the gate in one night.
7. **Ch 1's wood and table move into Arrival.** 1 and 2 are now the Prologue, so Act I starts
   at iron. *Recommend: yes.*
8. **Reward cuts on moved quests.** 430: 2 golden apples + an Uncommon bag → 1 + a Common bag.
   61: 3 Common + 1 Uncommon → 2 Common. 0: the bed moves to 471. Small, and all toward the
   Prologue band. *Recommend: yes.*
9. **The voice.** Approve the section 4 sample (or mark what to change) before WP-10 spreads
   it over the other chapters.
10. **Zombie Pigmen count.** The hunt uses BQ's default `subtypes=1`, so Zombie Pigmen (a
    subclass) count toward the five. Harmless on night one. *Recommend: leave it.*

## 6. What was checked, and what was not

Done:
- `qb.py check --book tools/quests/drafts/DefaultQuests.wp5.json` passes: lint, ids, cheese,
  stack, text, hunt, path. The warnings are the five the shipped book already has, plus the
  "Ch 5: Dark Paths" tab name. Every item ID is in `registry.json`.
- `qb.py path` on the draft: every numbered chapter still opens strictly after the one
  before; 322 quests, all reachable.
- The patch applies cleanly to a scratch copy of the **server** book too (it is the older
  book with HBM), and passes there apart from the HBM IDs that only that book carries.
- Re-running the patch on the finished draft changes no bytes.

Not done, and needed before the book ships:
- **A slim server boot of the draft** (WP-5's validation): `Loaded 322 quests`, no
  placeholders for the new items, and `/qfcensus find Zombie` to confirm the hunt target.
  `Zombie` is in `tools/quests/entities.json` marked "not yet checked".
- **A walk-through on a fresh world**, timed, and a look at the tab layout in game.
- A check that a Gravestone block given as a reward places as a decorative grave.

## 7. Applying it, once approved

One command applies the Prologue and the Ch 1 rewrite to both repo books:

```
py -3.10 -I tools/quests/patches/wp5_prologue.py --book all
```

Add `--skip-voice` to apply the Prologue without the Ch 1 rewrite. The patch bumps
`pack_version` to 9. Then the usual steps from `tools/quests/README.md`: `qb.py check`, the
slim server boot above, a commit, and on the server `/bq_admin default load`. Changes to the
decisions (a different reward, a dropped quest) are edits to the tables at the top of the
patch; rebuild the draft with `py -3.10 -I tools/quests/patches/wp5_prologue.py` and check it
again.
