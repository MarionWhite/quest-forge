(`pack/config/betterquesting/DefaultQuests.json`) and the server book are unchanged.

## 5. Voice: before and after

Colour codes are stripped. `<br>` is a paragraph break. Before is the WP-5 draft (WP-5 does not
touch these quests), after is the act draft. The **name** line shows a title change.

What changed, against audit 4.7:

- **Hooks** are 14 words or fewer in every quest of the act.
- **Asks** say what the task checks: "Have 4 Clay Jars" and "Kill the Hydra. A kill by anyone in
  your party counts for all of you", never "wear it" or "build it" for a retrieval.
- **Group fights** say "you and your party" (15, 16, 17, 93).
- **Invented facts are gone or tagged.** The draft's claims that the book, the lang or the class
  files do not support were cut: "four flowers" (14), "mazestone will not yield" (93), "destroy
  the enchanted artifacts" (16), "seeds beside the wheat" (96), "the scream hurts anything
  nearby" (96). Section 7 lists the claims that stay and are not verified.
- **Quest 10 was misnamed.** "Make Mutandis" was the old text; its task checks 4 clay jars. The
  title and the text now say jars.
- **No meta-structure.** No "this page", "side tab" or "numbered chapter" in the act.
- **No exclamation marks** in the act's texts.

## 8. Applying it, once approved

One command applies the WP-5 Prologue and the Act II patch to both repo books:

```
py -3.10 -I tools/quests/patches/wp6_act2.py --book all
```

Then the usual steps from `tools/quests/README.md`: `qb.py check`, the slim server boot above, a
commit, and on the server `/bq_admin default load`. The patch sets `pack_version` to 10.

Changes to the decisions are edits to the tables at the top of `wp6_act2.py`: `ACT1_FINALE`,
`REPOINT`, `MOVES`, `NEW`, `REWARDS`, `TEXTS` and `NAMES`. Rebuild the drafts with the same
command without `--book`, then check the act draft again.
