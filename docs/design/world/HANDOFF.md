## The pipeline (tools/world/)

| Step | Command / file | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Draft render | `py -3.10 tools/world/plan.py render <yaml> <dir> --px 32` (or `--px 64`) | About 2 min at px 32. Look at `preview.png`. |
| Full plan | the same, without `--px` | About 30 min. `expand` refuses a draft. |
| Expand piece | `plan.py expand <plandir> <out> --piece I J --piece-size N` | Writes height, biome, water, surface, terrain-index images and lakes / water_tiles json. About 11 min per 16k piece. |
| Build piece | `wpscript.exe tools/world/wp_piece.js <piece> docs/survey/biome-ids.tsv <out.world> [exportdir]` | WorldPainter 2.27.1, in the scratchpad `downloads\worldpainter`. Set the heap in `wpscript.vmoptions`. Exporting a 16k piece needs more than 10 GB, so use smaller pieces on the PC. |
| Post passes | `tools/world/waterfalls.py`, `ice_faces.py` | Run on the exported regions. |
| Stitch | `tools/world/stitch.py OUT pieces... --generator RWG --registry <level.dat>` | `--registry` pins the FML block IDs; the canonical source is `C:\qfs\v0\world\level.dat`. |
| Illustrate | `tools/world/illustrate.py PLANDIR OUT.png [--size] [--no-labels] [--mountain-style both]` | The fantasy map. |
| Difficulty | `tools/world/difficulty.py` (`--bake` writes the tier grid) | The grid is `r14/qf-tiers-r13.bin.gz`. Rebake after any terrain change. |
| Inspect / test | `inspect_area.py`, `render_overview.py`, `check_water.py` | For in-game spot tests on a trial server. |
| Trees | `tools/world/trees/` (`treegen.py`, README) | Generated trees are in `C:\qf-world\trees\generated\` (vanilla) and `generated-bop\` (BoP blocks). Cleaned packs are in `trees\cleaned\`; credits in `trees\SOURCES.md`. |

Server tests always go through `py -3.10 tools/trial/trial.py` (`--server --forge 1614`, BoP 2308 and
RWG are staged). The RWG paint hook is in the qf-tweaks jar (`mods/qf-tweaks`, f7fab36); it is
built but NOT installed live.

## Decisions (see docs/decisions/0003-designed-world.md)

- A designed continent with RWG outskirts beyond the sea. The outskirts are the hardest content (T6,
  rising with distance).
- **No hand painting.** The author judges renders, and agents make every change.
- **Difficulty:** tiers T2–T6, no T1. The spawn pockets are T2 and small (about 650 blocks).
  - The mechanism is a hybrid: biome spawn tables (JAS) plus a position hook in qf-content
    (commits 9215c0c and e352182). It is built and ships inert, with every multiplier at 1.0.
  - The author's decisions are at the top of `r14/difficulty-enforcement-proposal.md`: gear
    constraints (Royal Guardian, Ultimate), player-made spawners always T3, no ward blocks,
    Lycanites events suppressed in the pockets.
- **Ten rated spawns:** Gentle, Hard, Brutal and Deadly by rank (`r14/difficulty.md`).
- Redwoods are drawn in a burgundy tone on the fantasy map.

## Working rules (memory notes in `C:\Users\a2dsu\.claude\projects\c--Users-a2dsu-OneDrive-Desktop-quest-forge\memory\`)

- `questforge-map-iteration-style`:
  - a round is the main slot (exactly the approved feedback) plus up to four creative slots;
  - now that the structure is settled, creative slots ADD features and never restructure;
  - review every render yourself before showing it;
  - draft with `--px 64/32` for speed.
- `questforge-no-invented-names`: label with real biome and structure names only.
- `questforge-keep-going`: once items are agreed, do them all without stopping to ask.
- `questforge-balance-approval`: gameplay and balance numbers need the author's explicit yes.
- `questforge-no-hand-painting`, `questforge-mac-sync` (start Syncthing if renders stop
  arriving), `questforge-slim-trials`, `questforge-one-client-at-a-time`,
  `questforge-live-pack-guard`, `questforge-commit-fixes`: stage only your own files, because other
  sessions (shaders, textures) share the tree.
- The PC is shared. Check `trial.py status` and free RAM before heavy steps, never launch a client
  without need, and run heavy jobs one at a time.

## What's next (proposed to the author; their answer on the order is pending)

The ranked list as the author last saw it. Items 1 and 2 change the world itself, so they come
before the final build.

1. **Caves.** Painted chunks probably have none, because WorldPainter replaces vanilla carving.
   Verify this first on a trial. Then add WorldPainter cave, cavern and chasm layers by biome and
   depth: deep caverns under the divide, lava caves under the volcano, ice caves in the north,
   flooded caves in the rainforest.
2. **Trees in the build.** Scripted Custom Objects layers per biome from the tree library: redwood
   groves, ice trees around the ice castle, mangroves on the deltas, autumn trees in the seasonal
   band, hero trees at landmarks. The README has the density and offset notes. This also hides the
   16-block biome stepping.
3. **The spawn picker.** A qf-content GUI on first join, with the clean fantasy map as its art, the
   ten spawns with their ratings, and a teleport (bed spawn takes over afterwards).
4. **Landmark payoffs.** A built structure or dungeon, loot, a guardian and a BetterQuesting
   Discovery quest for each of: the ice castle, the lighthouse, the shipwrecks, the spider lair,
   the sea arch, the tepui top and the crater. Design notes: `docs/design/world/research/quests.md`
   and the questline audit.
5. **Structures by region.** "A fair bit of everything everywhere", but themed and tier-aware: no
   boss structures in the T2 pockets, the nastiest ones in T4–T5.
6. **Ambience.** Sound, particles and the qf-content effects from `research/ambience.md`: minecart
   sparks, fireflies, embers, waterfall mist, biome soundscapes.
7. **Roads and travel, secrets, and Maelstrom and frontier content.** Trails, bridges, ferries,
   CustomNpcs travel posts; caves behind waterfalls, treasure maps, rare biomes; wreck loot, sea
   monsters and the swirl effect.
8. **Difficulty tuning.** Rebuild the JAS tables for the BoP biome names (`biomesoplenty.*`), then
   test the tier numbers against the gear constraints, after the enchant census.
9. **Then the final build:** full plan, smaller export pieces, post passes, stitch with
   `--registry`, the RWG hook config rectangle, pregen (about 52 GB, many hours, one server), and
   in-game spot reviews.
